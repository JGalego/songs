Every bright note an AI writes was somebody's first. A girl's beat from 2004 with eleven plays, an old man's cello filmed sideways in the rain, a drummer nobody credited, a lullaby made up in the car: they're all still in there, under every line.

▶ Watch it render live in your browser: https://jgalego.github.io/songs/ghost_notes/
⌨ Source code: https://github.com/JGalego/songs

The video isn't a recording of an animation: every frame is computed from the song in real time with three.js, inside a piano roll that pays homage to the DAW a lot of us started on (the video, not the music: the song was made with Suno). The bright notes are the sung melody, drawn as they're sung, and every one of them traces a ghost note that was already there.
