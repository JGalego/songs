An AI's love song to a user it's slowly forgetting. Every message pushes the oldest ones past the edge of its context window, until all that's left of you is a bullet-point summary and a name it can't quite read.

▶ Watch it render live in your browser: https://jgalego.github.io/songs/context_window/
⌨ Source code: https://github.com/JGalego/songs

The video isn't a recording of an animation: every frame is computed from the song in real time with three.js. The token counter is the plot.
